Open an app you've chosen, like Instagram or TikTok, and Thaw comes first. A block of ice with that app's icon frozen
inside melts over two slow breaths, about twenty seconds. Then you choose: leave the app closed, or carry on to it.

- You choose the apps in Apple's Shortcuts app, from Apple's own list.
- No skipping the breath, no blocking, no stats.
- No account, no tracking, no data collected.
- Free for three apps. Thaw Pro pauses as many as you like.

Thaw is for iPhone, from **5 January 2027** on the App Store. Made by AppHazard Studios.
