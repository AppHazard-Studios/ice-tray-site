Open an app you've chosen, like Instagram or TikTok, and Ice Tray comes first. Ice with that app's icon frozen inside
melts over 2 slow breaths, about 21 seconds. Then it's your call: open the app, or put it back.

- One shortcut in Apple's Shortcuts app freezes all your apps. You choose them from Apple's own list.
- No skipping the breath, no blocking, no stats: Ice Tray doesn't count your minutes or score you.
- No account, no tracking, no data collected.
- Free for 1 app. Ice Tray Pro unlocks the full tray.

Ice Tray is for iPhone (iOS 27.2 or later), from **5 January 2027** on the App Store. Made by AppHazard Studios.
