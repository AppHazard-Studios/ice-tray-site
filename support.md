# Support

## Set up Ice Tray

1. Open Ice Tray and follow the set-up. It adds Ice Tray's shortcut to Apple's Shortcuts app.
2. When Shortcuts asks which apps to freeze, pick yours, then tap **Add Shortcut**.
3. Open one of those apps from your Home Screen. It's frozen until you melt it.

To add more apps later, tap **Add Apps** in Ice Tray. Ice Tray needs iOS 27.2 or later.

## If an app doesn't freeze

First, check the app is in the shortcut: tap **Add Apps** in Ice Tray, which opens it.

If the shortcut still doesn't work, set it up again:

1. In Shortcuts, touch and hold the **Ice Tray** shortcut, then tap **Delete**.
2. Delete Ice Tray from your Home Screen, reinstall it from the App Store, and follow the set-up again.

Keep only one Ice Tray shortcut. With two, every app freezes twice.

## Restore Ice Tray Pro

On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling Ice Tray: open Ice Tray, tap the gear, then **Ice Tray Pro**, then **Restore
Purchases**. Use the Apple Account you bought it with. This also works for anyone in your Family Sharing group.

## Cancel Ice Tray Pro

In Ice Tray, tap the gear, then **Manage Subscription**. Or on your iPhone, go to **Settings**, tap your name, then
**Subscriptions**, then **Ice Tray Pro**.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your subscription renews. Ice Tray Pro stays on until the end of the month you've paid
for. Ice Tray Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase, so there's nothing to cancel.

Apple handles payments and refunds. To ask for a refund, go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
