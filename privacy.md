# Ice Tray privacy policy

_Last updated 13 November 2026_

Ice Tray is an iPhone app made by AppHazard Studios ("we"). **Ice Tray doesn't collect any data about you.**

- **No account and no sign-in.**
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking.** Ice Tray contains no third-party code that does any of this.
- **Nothing reaches us.** Ice Tray has no server. Your shortcut hands it the icons of the apps you freeze; for apps on
  the App Store it asks Apple for a sharper one, and buying Ice Tray Pro goes through Apple too (all below).

## What stays on your iPhone

Ice Tray keeps these things on your iPhone, in storage only Ice Tray and its Shortcuts action can read:

- your Haptics and Appearance settings,
- whether you've seen the first breath, and whether you've opened the link to add Ice Tray's shortcut,
- the apps you freeze: for each app your shortcut hands Ice Tray, its name and its bundle ID (the ID every app has,
  like `com.apple.podcasts` for Podcasts), in the order Ice Tray first met it, so it can list them on home and tell
  one app from another,
- which 1 is your free app, and with Ice Tray Pro which apps you've switched off,
- the apps you've removed from home, so Ice Tray doesn't freeze them or list them again by itself,
- for each of those apps, the time you last chose Open, so it stays unfrozen for 2 minutes,
- how many times each day you reached for a frozen app and put it back, for the last 8 weeks, so home can tell you your
  week,
- the icons of those apps: the picture your shortcut hands over, and the sharper one from Apple's App Store (below),
- whether you have Ice Tray Pro, and whether it's a subscription you bought yourself (a yes or no each).

They never reach us, and deleting Ice Tray deletes them. The icon pictures never leave your iPhone. The only thing
that does is each app's bundle ID (or, for an app Ice Tray only knows by name, its name) with your region, sent to
Apple to ask for a sharper icon.

## Shortcuts

You choose which apps to freeze in Apple's Shortcuts app, by adding Ice Tray's shortcut and picking apps from Apple's
list. The shortcut belongs to you and lives in Shortcuts. Adding it from Ice Tray's iCloud link is between you and
Apple; it doesn't tell us who you are.

When the shortcut runs, its Text step tells Ice Tray the name and bundle ID of the app being opened, and its Icon step
hands over the app's icon. Those stay on your iPhone as above, apart from the sharper-icon request below. Ice Tray
hands your choice (Open or Put Back) back to the shortcut, which opens the app with Apple's Open App action or goes to
the Home Screen. All of that happens on your iPhone.

## App icons

Your shortcut hands Ice Tray each app's icon, so the ice holds the real icon from its first moment, for Apple's own
apps too. For apps from the App Store, Ice Tray also asks Apple for a sharper one. It asks in the background, after the
ice has landed, for any app whose sharper icon is missing or more than 30 days old.

Each request is Apple's App Store lookup for one app's bundle ID: it sends only that bundle ID and your iPhone's
region. (For an app Ice Tray only knows by name, from an older or hand-made shortcut, it sends the name instead.) No
cookies, no account, nothing else about you. It goes to Apple, not to us. As with any request, Apple can see the
internet address it came from; Apple's privacy policy covers that. Where the App Store has nothing (some of Apple's
own apps), the app keeps the icon your shortcut handed over.

The icon pictures your shortcut hands over, and the ones from the App Store, stay on your iPhone, in Ice Tray's caches.
They are shown only inside Ice Tray. Removing an app on home deletes its icon. If an app has no icon (an older or
hand-made shortcut, no connection and no icon yet), Ice Tray shows a frosted tile with the app's first letter instead.

## Ice Tray Pro

Ice Tray Pro is bought, restored and managed through Apple's App Store with your Apple Account. Apple handles the
payment: Ice Tray never sees your payment details, your Apple Account or who you are. Ice Tray only keeps whether you
have Ice Tray Pro. Apple's privacy policy covers the purchase itself.

## If you email us

If you write to us, we see your email address and what you send, and use them only to reply. We don't add you to a
list or pass your email on. Ask us to delete your messages and we will.

## This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no cookies, no analytics and no forms. GitHub, like any web host, sees
the internet address of each visit; [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
covers that.

## Children

Ice Tray collects nothing from anyone, children included.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be here with a new date. If Ice Tray ever needed to collect anything, the
policy, the App Store's privacy label and the app would say so first.
