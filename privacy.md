# Privacy Policy

<p class="updated">Last updated: 23 November 2026</p>

This Privacy Policy explains how AppHazard Studios ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the Ice Tray
app for iPhone (the "App") and this website (the "Site").

<div class="summary" markdown="1">
**Summary.** We don't collect personal information through the App. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising
and no tracking. The information the App needs to work stays on your iPhone, where we can't see it.
</div>

## Who we are

Ice Tray is made by AppHazard Studios, an independent developer based in Australia. AppHazard Studios is responsible
for any personal information described in this policy. You can contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

## Information we collect

We don't collect personal information through the App. The App has no account or sign-in, and we don't run a server
for it. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, from us or from anyone else. Its App Store privacy
label is "Data Not Collected".

We receive personal information only if you choose to email us (see [If you contact us](#if-you-contact-us)).

## Information stored on your device

To work, the App keeps some information on your iPhone, in a private storage area that only the App and its Shortcuts
action can read (an App Group). It isn't sent to us, and we can't access it. It includes:

- the apps you add to Ice Tray: each app's name, its bundle identifier (the ID every app has, such as
  com.apple.podcasts) and its icon;
- your choices for those apps, such as which one is your free app and, with Ice Tray Pro, which ones are switched off;
- when you last chose to open each app, and how many times today you reached for it;
- a record of when you reached for a frozen app and whether you opened it, kept for 8 weeks so the App can show you a
  summary of your week;
- your settings, how far you've got through set-up, and whether you have Ice Tray Pro.

Like other app data, this information may be included in your iPhone's backups. Deleting the App deletes it from your
iPhone.

## Information shared with Apple

Some features of the App use Apple's services. When they do, Apple receives the information, not us, and
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) applies.

- **App icons.** To show a sharp icon for an app from the App Store, the App asks Apple's public App Store lookup for
  that app's icon. Each request sends only the app's bundle identifier (or, for some apps, its name) and your iPhone's
  region. It includes no account, cookie or other identifier, although, as with any internet request, Apple can see
  the IP address it comes from. The icons are saved on your iPhone.
- **Shortcuts.** The App works through a shortcut in Apple's Shortcuts app, which you add and control. The shortcut
  runs on your iPhone and tells the App which app is being opened. That information stays on your iPhone.
- **Purchases.** Ice Tray Pro is bought, restored and managed through the App Store, and Apple processes the payment.
  We don't receive your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. The App only learns whether you have Ice
  Tray Pro.

## How we use information

The App uses the information on your device only to provide its features, such as listing your apps, freezing them
and showing your weekly summary. We use emails you send us only to reply to you. We don't use any information for
marketing or advertising.

## How we share information

We don't sell, rent or share personal information. We would disclose an email you sent us only if the law required
us to.

## If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if your email includes it, and what you write. We use it
only to reply to you. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), so your message is stored on Google's servers, which may
be outside your country.

## How long we keep information

Information on your device stays there until you delete the App, except the record of your reaches, which the App
keeps for 8 weeks. We keep emails for as long as we need them to help you, and we'll delete them if you ask.

## Security

The information the App keeps is protected by the security built into iOS, which keeps each app's storage separate
and encrypts your iPhone's data.

## Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object
to or restrict how it's used, and to receive a copy of it. These rights come from laws such as Australia's Privacy Act
1988, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Because we don't collect personal information through the App, the only personal information we may hold is email
you've sent us. To make a request about it, email us and we'll respond within 30 days. You control the information on
your device yourself: you can remove apps in Ice Tray, or delete the App.

- **EEA and UK:** our legal basis for handling your emails is our legitimate interest in replying to you.
- **California:** we don't sell or share personal information, as the CCPA defines those terms.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to your
local regulator: in Australia, the [Office of the Australian Information Commissioner](https://www.oaic.gov.au); in
the EEA or UK, your data protection authority.

## Children

Ice Tray is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you
believe a child has emailed us, contact us and we'll delete the message.

## This website

The Site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or forms. GitHub, which runs the servers, records
visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in
[GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll post the new version on this page and change the date
at the top. If the App ever starts collecting personal information, we'll update this policy and the App Store privacy
label before it does.
