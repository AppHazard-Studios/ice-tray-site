## What Ice Tray is, and isn't

Ice Tray freezes apps you choose: before one opens, it plays a short guided breath, then lets you decide whether to open
it. It's a gentle moment, not a blocker: you can always open the app.

Ice Tray isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If breathing exercises make you
dizzy or uncomfortable, stop, and breathe normally. Ice Tray can't tell whether you breathe along, and doesn't try.

Ice Tray works through Apple's Shortcuts app, which you set up and control. iOS, Shortcuts and the apps you freeze are
made by others and can change; we'll do our best to keep Ice Tray working with them, but can't promise it always will.

## Free and Ice Tray Pro

- **Free:** the freeze for 1 app (the first app your shortcut hands Ice Tray, or the one you choose on home).
- **Ice Tray Pro** freezes every app you choose, with a switch for each on Ice Tray's home to let it open normally.
  It's sold in Ice Tray through Apple's App Store as:
  - **Ice Tray Pro Monthly:** an auto-renewing subscription, 1 month, US$0.99 a month.
  - **Ice Tray Pro Lifetime:** a single purchase, US$9.99, with nothing to renew.

  Prices are shown in your own currency before you buy, and may differ by country. There is no free trial.

### Auto-renewing subscriptions

- Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
- A subscription renews automatically for the same length and price unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before
  the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24
  hours before the period ends.
- Manage or cancel in Ice Tray (Settings → Manage Subscription) or on your iPhone (Settings → your name →
  Subscriptions). After cancelling, Ice Tray Pro lasts until the end of the period you've paid for.
- If a price ever goes up, Apple tells you first and, where the law or Apple requires it, asks you to agree.

### Lifetime

Ice Tray Pro Lifetime is Ice Tray Pro for as long as Ice Tray is available for your Apple Account, on the devices that account
uses, with Family Sharing.

### Family Sharing, restoring, refunds

- Both plans can be shared with your Family Sharing group.
- **Restore** (Ice Tray → Settings → Ice Tray Pro → Restore Purchases) brings Ice Tray Pro back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.
- Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
